This policy explains how Anikara (anikara.co.uk) uses cookies and similar technologies, and the choices you have.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly and can also be used to remember your preferences.

How we use cookies

We keep cookie use on this site to a minimum. Currently, we use:

EssentialRemembers your cookie preference so we don't show the banner againStored until you clear your browser data

We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. If this changes in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

Your choices

When you first visit our site, you can choose to accept or reject optional cookies via the banner shown. You can also clear cookies at any time through your browser settings, which will reset your preference.

Third-party services

Our contact form is processed by Formspree, a third-party service. Formspree may set its own cookies as part of processing your submission — please see Formspree's privacy policy for details.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our site or services change. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
